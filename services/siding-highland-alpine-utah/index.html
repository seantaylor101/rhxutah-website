Frequently Asked Questions

How much does siding installation cost in Utah?

Siding installation in Utah typically runs $6–$15 per square foot installed, depending on material and the condition of the existing exterior. A full siding replacement on an average-sized home usually falls between $12,000–$30,000. Ridgeline provides a free on-site quote with an exact price before any work begins.

Do I need a permit for siding replacement in Highland and Alpine?

Most Utah County cities, including Highland and Alpine, require a permit for siding replacement. Ridgeline can help confirm requirements for your specific property before work begins.

What's the difference between vinyl, LP SmartSide, and fiber cement siding?

Vinyl siding is the most affordable and low-maintenance, but has a shorter lifespan. LP SmartSide is engineered wood with a traditional look and strong impact resistance. Fiber cement is the most durable and fire-resistant, with a higher upfront cost but the longest lifespan.
