Exterior Services Built for Ogden Homes

When it comes to your home’s exterior, Ogden’s climate demands quality you can count on. At Ridgeline Home Exteriors, we specialize in the full range of exterior services that keep your home looking great and performing even better — from harsh winters to blazing summers.

Our siding services offer both Vinyl and James Hardie options in a variety of styles and colors, giving your home a fresh, durable finish that stands up to the elements. Whether you’re updating a single-family home or a commercial property, our expert team handles every installation with precision and care.

Keep water where it belongs with our gutter solutions — including professional gutter guard installation and routine gutter cleaning. We help Ogden homeowners avoid costly water damage by ensuring rainwater flows freely away from your roof, walls, and foundation all year long.

Our in-ground drain systems take water management one step further, redirecting runoff away from your home’s foundation to protect your landscaping and prevent structural damage. Custom-designed for your property, these low-maintenance systems deliver lasting peace of mind.

For a timeless, low-maintenance look, our stucco services provide a beautiful and weather-resistant exterior finish for both new construction and re-coats — adding curb appeal and long-term value to any property.

When winter hits the Wasatch Front, ice dams can be a serious threat. Our trained technicians use professional-grade equipment to safely remove ice dams without damaging your roof or gutters — protecting your home from costly water infiltration.

And when unexpected damage occurs, our repair and prevention services have you covered. From broken siding and wood rot to soffit, fascia, and smashed downspouts, we restore your home’s exterior quickly and correctly. We also work directly with insurance companies to help make the claims process as smooth as possible.
